THE RUGBY ROBUST PRINCIPLE

Availability to train equals availability to perform.

Robustness is the capacity to consistently engage in training and games with maximal effort — built through education, movement preparation, neuromuscular training and progressive physical capacity.

What is Rugby Robust? Not just injury prevention. Preparation for the demands of rugby.

Rugby Robust combines our understanding of youth rugby injuries, neuromuscular training principles and the demands of the sport into one practical system players can understand and use.

Who leads it? Delivered by George Gauntlett Physio, with 8 years in senior and academy men’s rugby.

The practical framework

Move. Control. Build. React.

Each area is progressed from movement options, into control and physical capacity, then into more reactive rugby-relevant challenges.

01

MOVE

Develop the mobility and movement options needed to access useful rugby positions before adding more control, load or speed.

02

CONTROL

Position, balance, coordination and neuromuscular control before adding more load or speed.

03

BUILD

Develop strength and capacity so key tissues and movement patterns can tolerate repeated rugby demands.

04

REACT

Add decision-making, perturbation, partner interaction and speed to bridge preparation towards the game.
